# A typical bicycle build day

Bike build days are special. They can be a very long day and a lot of work, yet very rewarding nevertheless. The schedule vary whildly depending on the how far the team has to travel. Often it is 1 to 2.5hrs away, so the mornings are early in order to beat the heat of the day.

During a build, bicycles go through 3 main stations:

1. Pre-assmbly - where bicycles are unloaded, handle bars and seats are adjusted, peddals are installed and valve stems are replaced
2. Assembly - baskets are installed, complete instpection of the bicycle, minor fixes are completed
3. QA - defects identified by Assembly are repaired, and second inspection is completed (including test drive)

A typical build day is something like:

- 05:00 Wakeup __(Weather: 25C)__
- 05:30 Breakfast
- 06:00 Bus departs
- 08:00 Arrival at school or community centre
- 08:05 Scope out the site, identify work areas and unload the bus.
- 08:15 Work begins! Start turning wrenches __(Weather: 30C)__
- 11:00 All bikes complete `Pre-assembly` phase (workers re-allocated to QA)
- 12:00 All bikes complete `Assembly` phase (workers prepare for distribution, train teachers about water purifcation systems, etc)
- 12:?? Working lunch __(Weather: 35C)__
- 13:30 Children are assembled; presentations begin. This includes oral hygiene demonstration, and remarks by local officials.
- 14:00 All bikes have completed repair in `QA` and bikes are ready for distribution
- 14:00 Bike distribution - Kids sign pledges to use their bicycle for school; Photos taken with each child and their new bike
- 15:00 Site cleanup & pack-up the bus; team debrief
- 15:05 Depart from build site
- 17:00 Arrival back at hotel __(Weather: 30C)__
- 18:00 Dinner
- 21:00 Bed
